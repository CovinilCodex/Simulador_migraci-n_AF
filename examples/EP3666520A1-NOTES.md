# EP 3 666 520 A1: PET/EVA/PET approximation

Import `EP3666520A1-PET-EVA-PET.json` with the application's Import button. Both cases are simulated automatically. A is 3°C and B is 25°C; both run for 720 h. Existing settings are replaced by the imported comparison.

Source: user-supplied Cryovac patent EP3666520A1, Table 1 (printed p.15), Table 2 (p.16), paragraphs 0213–0222 and Table 3 (pp.17–18).

Example 1 is actually four layers: 2 µm PETG sealant / 3 µm (70% modified EMA + 30% MB1) / 10 µm PET/PETG blend / 2 µm PET-based outer layer. MB1 contains 10% AF, hence 3 wt% active AF in the 3 µm layer. The comparison collapses the last two layers into a 12 µm PET backing and uses EVA as the requested proxy for the entire central blend. Neither PET layer initially contains AF.

The sealant uses the app's PET HSP and diffusivity as an explicit approximation for PETG, with Tg=78°C and no Tm (amorphous). The backing uses PET transport defaults with Tg=78°C and Tm=245°C reported for PET1. The EVA layer retains the application's illustrative EVA density, HSP, diffusion and transitions. EVA is not asserted to have the properties of EMA or its masterbatch blend. GMS is a representative fatty-acid ester selected from the library; the patent does not identify MB1's exact AF molecules. These substitutions require experimental calibration.

Face A is the thin sealant side facing the product. It uses continuous wash with the default assumed rate 0.15 h^-1. Face B is dry and can adsorb/desorb but cannot lose mass externally. Surface capacity (2 mg/m²), adsorption and desorption are also uncalibrated defaults. This is a depletion sensitivity comparison, NOT a reproduction of the patent test. Change Face A to dry and the horizon to 24 h to examine loss-free inventory development, but even that does not simulate condensation or optical AF scoring.

The patent tests at 2–4°C, without direct liquid contact; Example 1 scores 5 at 1, 3 and 24 h, including after a 100°C/4 s external hot-plate treatment. These results do not establish a 30-day wash lifetime. The simulator starts with zero surface inventory and does not model migration during extrusion, orientation, cooling or storage before testing. Thus failure to reproduce immediate cold AF performance would not refute the patent.

Paragraphs 0140–0143 describe an optional extra AF-bearing layer 2′ explicitly acting as a reservoir feeding layer 2. This file represents the simpler single AF-bearing inner layer requested by the user. Paragraphs 0148–0150 emphasize sealant thickness and the thickness/loading of the AF-bearing layer. They support examining delivery as well as retention.

No measured AF efficacy threshold is supplied by the patent. Surface inventory and retained percentage must not be labeled functional survival without calibration to an AF test. The solver and general application defaults are unchanged; this is an importable, reproducible scenario.
